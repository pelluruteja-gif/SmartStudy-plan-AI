Core Priority Algorithm & Analysis

Multi-factor priority calculation evaluating Urgency, Difficulty, and Preparation Gap.

High Priority (≥ 70) Medium Priority (≥ 45) Low Priority (< 45)
1. Exam Urgency (40%) Weight: 0.40

Exponential urgency curve based on days remaining until the exam date.

2. Difficulty Score (25%) Weight: 0.25

Easy = 20 • Medium = 50 • Hard = 80.

3. Preparation Gap (35%) Weight: 0.35

Calculated as: 100 - Current Preparation %.

Priority = (Urgency × 0.40) + (Difficulty × 0.25) + (PreparationGap × 0.35)
